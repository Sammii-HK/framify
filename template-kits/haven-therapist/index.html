How I work

Warm, honest, and led by what you need

I’m trained in several approaches and use what helps, rather than fitting you to a method.

Relational

The relationship between us is part of the work. You can expect someone who listens closely, is straight with you, and doesn’t judge.

Practical when it helps

CBT-informed tools for anxiety, sleep and overthinking, so you leave sessions with something to try as well as something to think about.

Trauma-aware

We go at a pace that feels safe. You choose what we talk about, and you can always say “not today”.

What to expect

Starting is the hardest part. Here’s how it goes.

  1. 1

    A free 15-minute call

    A relaxed chat by phone or video. You tell me a little about what’s going on, ask anything you like, and we see if it feels right.

  2. 2

    Your first session

    We talk about what brings you here and what you’d like to be different. There’s no pressure to commit to more.

  3. 3

    Working together

    Usually weekly, at the same time. We check in every six sessions on how it’s going and whether to change anything.

Questions

Things people often ask

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on what you bring. Some people come for six to eight sessions with a specific focus; others stay longer to work on deeper patterns. We’ll review together regularly, and you can stop whenever you want.

Is what I say confidential?

Yes, with a few limits I’ll explain in our first session: if I believe you or someone else is at serious risk of harm, or if the law requires me to share information. I’d always try to talk to you first.

Online or in person: which is better?

Both work well. Online suits busy schedules and people who feel more at ease at home. Some people prefer the separation of coming to a room. You can switch between them.

What if it doesn’t feel right?

Please tell me. The fit between you and your therapist matters more than almost anything else. If I’m not the right person, I’ll help you find someone who might be.

Do you work with health insurers?

I’m registered with several insurers. Check with yours whether you need a referral first, and I’ll send you everything they need.
