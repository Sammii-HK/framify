FAQ

Good questions

Do I need to be home?

No. Most customers leave a key or a lockbox code. Keys are coded, never labelled with your address, and kept securely.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes, including a vacuum and eco-friendly products. If you’d rather we used yours, just say.

Are your cleaners vetted and insured?

Every cleaner is interviewed, reference-checked and background-checked, and we carry public liability insurance.

What if I need to skip or cancel?

Give us 48 hours’ notice and there’s no charge. There are no contracts; you can stop whenever you like.

I have pets. Is that OK?

Of course. Let us know about them when you book so we can match you with a cleaner who’s comfortable with animals.
