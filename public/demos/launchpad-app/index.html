// FAQ

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start planning. Sync uses your own iCloud or Google account, so there’s nothing to sign up for.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

It’s gone from your phone. If you turned on sync, you can delete the synced copy from Settings > Morrow > Delete synced data.

Can I cancel Pro any time?

Yes. Subscriptions are managed by the App Store or Google Play, and you can cancel in your account settings at any time.

Is there an iPad or Mac version?

Morrow runs on iPad today. A Mac version is on the roadmap.
