How can I help?

Morrow is made by one person. I read every message and usually reply within two working days.

Email support@example.com

Restore a purchase

Open Morrow, go to Settings > Morrow Pro and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google. On iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions.

Delete your data

Everything lives on your device. Deleting the app deletes your plans. If you turned on sync, go to Settings > Morrow > Delete synced data first.

Report a bug

Email me with your device model, OS version and Morrow version (shown at the bottom of Settings). A screenshot helps a lot.
